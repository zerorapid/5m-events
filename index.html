OUR PROCESS

Our 6-Step Execution Process

A structured, end-to-end operational roadmap designed to turn creative briefs into flawless live show experiences.

01. BRIEFING

Discovery & Briefing

Requirements capture, audience demographic profiling, and creative vision mapping.

Deliverable: Concept Deck & Scope
02. DESIGN

3D Spatial Design

Photorealistic 3D CAD stage renders, acoustic modeling, and lighting plots.

Deliverable: 3D Stage Renderings
03. COMPLIANCE

Venue & Permitting

Fire safety clearances, municipal permits, hotel liaison, and load-in timelines.

Deliverable: Permitted Site Plan
04. PRODUCTION

Fabrication & Rigging

Custom trussing, floral sculpture, LED wall integration, and sound check.

Deliverable: Calibrated Stage
05. SHOW DAY

Show Day Execution

Live showcalling, minute-by-minute artist cues, broadcast, and VIP hospitality.

Deliverable: Flawless Live Runbook
06. WRAP

Post-Event Deliverables

Same-day social media edits, guest photo/video delivery, and event debrief.

Deliverable: Final Media Package
