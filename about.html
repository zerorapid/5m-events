Our Story

A crew built on craft, collaboration, and care.

BKU began with a simple conviction: that every gathering — whether a 50-guest wedding or a 5,000-attendee summit — deserves to be more than an event. It deserves to be an experience.

Since 2024, we've grown into a full-scale event management and creative production company. Our multidisciplinary crew turns briefs into immersive environments, camera-ready content, and seamless run-of-show — so you can enjoy the spotlight while everything just works.

From concept mood boards and 3D layouts to stage fabrication, lighting, sound, artist management, and post-event content — we deliver shows that look spectacular and run on rails.

Meet The Founder

Jaideep Raviprakash

Jaideep founded BKU with a vision to redefine how India experiences events. With a background spanning creative direction, production management, and brand storytelling, he brings a rare blend of artistic sensibility and operational rigor to every project.

His belief is simple: great events aren't just planned — they're authored. Under his leadership, BKU has grown from a passionate startup into a trusted partner for some of the most memorable weddings, launches, and celebrations in the country.

When he's not on set or in a production meeting, you'll find him scouting locations, studying global event trends, or mentoring the next generation of event storytellers.

Our Journey

Milestones along the way.

A brief look at how BKU has grown from a bold idea into a trusted name in event production.

01
2024 The Beginning

BKU is founded.

Started with a small crew and a big vision — to craft events that feel as intentional as they look spectacular. Our first project: an intimate destination wedding in Rajasthan.

02
2024 First Milestone

50 events in our first year.

From intimate gatherings to corporate summits, we proved that scale doesn't have to come at the cost of soul. Our client roster began to grow through word-of-mouth alone.

03
2025 The Crew Grows

Expanding the team.

We brought on specialists in PR, technical production, and content — building a multidisciplinary crew capable of handling every aspect of an event in-house.

04
2026 Today

Nationwide, and growing.

With 250+ events delivered, 180+ happy clients, and a network of 95+ trusted vendors, BKU is now a name associated with craft, care, and unforgettable experiences across India.
