Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how 5M Events collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information.

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you fill out an inquiry form, request an event quote, subscribe to our Journal newsletter, or enter a service agreement. This may include:

  • Personal Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone number, company name, and billing details.
  • Event Specification Data: Event dates, venue preferences, guest counts, design themes, and budget ranges.
  • Payment Details: Processed securely through PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways. 5M Events does not store raw credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Design, coordinate, and execute end-to-end event management services.
  • Process invoices, retainer payments, and send related financial receipts.
  • Send technical production updates, run-of-show schedules, and operational notices.
  • Respond to your comments, inquiries, and custom project requests.
  • Deliver curated Journal newsletters and industry insights (with your explicit consent).

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personally identifiable information to third parties. We may share necessary event parameters with vetted production partners, venue authorities, and caterers strictly to execute your event, provided those parties agree to keep your information confidential.

4. Cookies and Web Technologies

Our website utilizes essential cookies and analytics tools to evaluate page performance, smooth scroll interactions, and overall user experience. You may configure your browser settings to decline non-essential cookies; however, certain site features may function with reduced performance.

5. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical, organizational, and physical security measures to guard your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. All client information is stored on encrypted, access-restricted servers.

6. Your Rights

You maintain full rights to inspect, update, correct, or request the erasure of your personal data stored by 5M Events. To exercise these rights or request data removal, please contact our team at fifthmedia@gmail.com.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to third-party partners or venue websites. These external sites operate under independent privacy policies. 5M Events accepts no responsibility or liability for the content or privacy practices of external web resources.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect evolving legal or operational standards. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
